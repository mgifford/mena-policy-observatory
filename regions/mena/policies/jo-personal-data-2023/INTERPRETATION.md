# Ambiguity register: Jordan Personal Data Protection Law

**Prototype, not legal advice.** All items require review.

### JO-24-01: Commencement date

- **Source observation:** Article (1) states that the Law enters into force six months after publication in the Official Gazette.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** The publication date and resulting six-month commencement date are not established from the captured English translation.
- **Risk:** high; `temporal_uncertainty`
- **Status:** unresolved

### JO-24-02: Retroactive application

- **Source observation:** Article (3)(A) applies the Law to data collected or processed before it comes into force.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** The retroactive application to data processed before commencement needs a clause-level scope analysis.
- **Risk:** high; `retrospective_application`
- **Status:** unresolved
