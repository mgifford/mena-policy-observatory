# Ambiguity register: Federal Law No. 29 of 2006

**Prototype, not legal advice.** All items require review.

### AE-LAW29-01: Digital scope of Articles 7 and 20

- **Section:** Articles 7 and 20(2)
- **Source observation:** Article 7 covers expression, Braille, sign language, other communication means and information; Article 20(2) lists electronic texts, sign language, Braille, audio and multimedia.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** Whether Articles 7 and 20 apply to government websites, apps and digital services, or only to the formats and services expressly listed, requires legal review.
- **Risk:** high; `digital_scope`
- **Status:** unresolved

### AE-LAW29-02: Article 23 implementing instrument

- **Section:** Articles 22-23
- **Source observation:** Article 23 requires the competent authorities to set engineering standards and a Cabinet decision to issue controls and exemptions.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** The implementing Cabinet decision and technical standards contemplated by Article 23 are not captured; their content and current status are unresolved.
- **Risk:** high; `implementing_instrument`
- **Status:** unresolved

### AE-LAW29-03: Relationship to later instruments

- **Section:** Article 1 and related instruments
- **Source observation:** The Law, the 2018 Cabinet Resolution and the 2024 policy address different aspects of disability inclusion and digital accessibility.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** The relationship between this 2006 Law, Cabinet Resolution 43/2018 and the 2024 National Digital Accessibility Policy needs a consolidated reading.
- **Risk:** high; `instrument_relationship`
- **Status:** unresolved

### AE-LAW29-04: Arabic/English authority

- **Section:** Language expressions
- **Source observation:** The UAE Legislation platform provides an English translation and states that the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict.
- **Unresolved interpretation:** The official English translation and the Arabic text may differ; Arabic prevails and native legal review is outstanding.
- **Risk:** high; `translation_authority`
- **Status:** unresolved
