# Human review handbook

Regional experts lead source selection, legal interpretation, translation and publication scope. Review the agent's evidence independently: open official sources, locate clause directly and examine exceptions and amendments. Record disagreements rather than silently overwriting an interpretation.

## Roles
- Regional lead: chooses relevant questions, source priority, review assignments and safeguards.
- Source reviewer: verifies official publisher, snapshot, locator, versions.
- Legal specialist: evaluates applicability, authority, scope and contested interpretation.
- Language reviewer: evaluates Arabic/English/French/etc against original.
- Technical reviewer: validates machine representation and test coverage.
- Accessibility reviewer: tests bilingual pages and authoring workflow.

## Review signature
Store a stable reviewer identifier only with consent and affiliation if publicly disclosed; review date; findings; approved/rejected/needs-work; scope of review; source revision and packet checksum. Avoid invented credentials and endorsements.

## Disagreement policy
Preserve both interpretations, evidence and reason for dissent; mark executable output blocked if disagreement affects material conclusion. Agent cannot choose a legally authoritative reading based on token probability.
