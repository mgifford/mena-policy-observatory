# Candidate source leads

`candidate-sources.json` is copied from the earlier research pass. These links have **not** been retrieved or checked in the preparation of this toolkit. Its reported dates and titles are leads, not verified legal findings. Some URLs lead to ministry explanations rather than primary statutes.

After source capture, record exact official authoritative sources, and verify all fields before migration into `regions/mena/policies`. There are no authenticated full-text statutes included.

## Cross-domain discovery portals (not substitutes for national law)
- W3C WAI laws and policies: https://www.w3.org/WAI/policies/
- United Nations Treaty Collection: https://treaties.un.org/
- OHCHR treaty bodies: https://tbinternet.ohchr.org/
- UNCTAD cyberlaw tracker: https://unctad.org/page/cyberlaw-tracker-country-detail
- UNESCO AI readiness: https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ram
- OECD AI principles: https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
- WHO abortion policies database: https://abortion-policies.srhr.org/
- ITU standards portal: https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC/en
- EU law for future European region: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/

A discovery catalogue can contain outdated information. Preserve its own provenance and retrieval date.
